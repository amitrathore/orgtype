Why Orgtype?

The problem

Coordination chaos is killing productivity.

Modern organizations are drowning in complexity. As teams grow and tools multiply, alignment becomes a full-time job. Knowledge workers now spend up to 58% of their time on coordination rather than creation, trapped in meetings, navigating silos, or duplicating efforts.

Managers burn 35-50% of their week syncing stakeholders, updating dashboards, or translating strategy into action. Most companies reorganize every 2-3 years, yet 70% of those reorgs fail to deliver results and often cause 15-20% productivity dips during transitions.

Add to that the silent cost of lost knowledge: when employees leave or teams shift, up to 40% of institutional know-how disappears. The result? Millions in wasted time, misalignment, and operational drag every single year.

Discover Orgtype

The solution

Orgtype turns overhead into intelligence.

Orgtype introduces a radically new operating model: your organization as a living, AI-powered system. At its core is a digital twin: a programmable, continuously evolving model of your org's roles, workflows, knowledge, and goals.

The twin replaces brittle org charts and static processes with real-time orchestration, allowing teams and tools to stay aligned without constant meetings or reorgs. For a 100-person company, that can mean $6M-$8M per year in reclaimed productivity.

The impact is profound. Because Orgtype augments, not replaces, humans, it frees people to focus on what only they can do: create, decide, lead, and grow.
